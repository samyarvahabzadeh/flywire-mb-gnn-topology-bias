# Exploratory readability and plasticity controls

## B · Frozen representation

Use the real graph and fresh seed 4301 for neural initialization and cues. Construct balanced, fixed-size train, validation and held-out splits of 512, 256 and 512 cue presentations. Use the binary family's original layout and streams 404 and 405, with stream 407 for validation. Require pairwise disjoint exact PN patterns and sparse projected KC drives. Save input KCs, the complete vector of scalar MBON outputs, and the two current group means. Also inspect the full MBON pre-head embedding, the representation the shared B head receives. An unrestricted linear probe on the flattened embeddings can put different coefficients on different MBONs. The original B head ties its coefficients across neurons.

Standardize with training means and standard deviations only. Drop dimensions with train SD ≤ 1e−8. Select L2 = 0.001, 0.01, 0.1 or 1.0 by validation balanced accuracy, then validation log loss and stronger regularization. Keep the selected train fit for the final one-time evaluation. Report balanced accuracy and AUROC, dimensions, train feature scales, constant coordinates, native output saturation and the scalar fixed readout's dispersion. Treat fitted-probe accuracy as a readability check.

## C · Real KC→MBON edges and signed control

The previous arm C was an **offset-only approximation** on the GNN head. In this model, only the weight on each selected real KC→MBON11 pair is plastic. Use bilateral GABAergic MBON11 (MVP2, γ1pedc) and the corresponding same-side dopaminergic PPL101 identities. Check the exact roots, cell types and transmitter annotations. Keep same-side `KCg-` inputs to each MBON11. These are whole-neuron pair edges, used as a compartment proxy. The graph has no per-synapse compartment labels for this mask.

Hige et al. observed that pairing odor with PPL1-γ1pedc activation depresses the conditioned response of the γ1pedc MBON. Li et al. identifies this pathway as PPL101→plasticity of KC→MBON11, with the MBON11 depression favoring avoidance. The frozen GNN readout assigns a negative coefficient to these two MBONs, consistent with this independently sourced direction. In the reduced model the US is an **aversive** pulse. Decreasing MBON11 output raises the avoidance score. [Hige et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2015.11.003) · [Li et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.62576)

For each selected pair `(k,m)`, let `x_k` be the normalized real PN-derived KC input for the current cue. Initialize `w_km=1`. Apply `w_km ← clip(w_km − 0.05 R x_k, 0, 1)`, where `R=1` for the reinforced cue in training and otherwise `R=0`. Compute `u_m = Σ_k w_km x_k / Σ_k x_k` on its selected edges, with positive denominators required. The fixed avoidance score is `V = 0.25 + 0.5 × (1 − mean_m u_m)`. Cue-normalized averaging gives all cues the same untrained reference with equal initial weights. The fixed affine range stays away from BCE endpoints. The step and bounds use the old task engine's numerical scale. Jiang & Litwin-Kumar Eq. 4 combines a DAN-trace × KC term and a negative KC-trace × DAN term. This reduction implements the latter at discrete forward pairings, using within-cue activity and an external pulse. It omits recurrent DAN dynamics, separate eligibility traces and the time constant of induction. [Jiang & Litwin-Kumar (2021), Eq. 4](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1009205)

Draw the first fixed A+/A− pair from the existing real cue generator at seed 4302. Run 48 presentations of each cue in fixed order. Use independent weight starts in standard, no-US and swapped-US controls. Evaluate both cues without reinforcement. Pass only if the avoidance gap in favor of the reinforced cue is ≥0.01 after both standard and swapped training, the no-US arm changes no scores or weights, and precisely the edges with active reinforced presynaptic KCs change. Summarize cue-exclusive and shared activity separately. If it passes, evaluate the same frozen weights on 128 fresh instances per family from stream 405 with exact PN and projected-KC patterns held out. Report their raw score gap, balanced accuracy at the midpoint of the two conditioned training-cue scores, and rank AUROC. There is no extra training in either probe.

The future Adam comparator uses the identical real edge list, initialization, output function and bounds. Its only parameter is the same vector of KC→MBON weights. It computes BCE to the same cue labels with Adam (0.001) and projects weights into the same bounds after every update. This phase verifies parameter support only. It runs no Adam learning on real data.

## Null eligibility

`NullStepper.step` swaps postsynaptic targets of any compatible edges in A and of edges within the same source transmitter-sign stratum in B. Its validation preserves nodewise in/out degree and, for B, sign-stratified in/out degree. The swap predicate has no KC/MBON role, γ-subtype, side, exact pair or compartment constraint. Therefore neither variant guarantees preservation of the plastic edge mask or of the identity of the PPL101–MBON11 compartment. No null learning is scheduled.
